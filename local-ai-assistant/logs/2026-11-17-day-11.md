# Day 11 — Conversation Refactoring & Memory Reliability

**Date:** November 16–17, 2026

## Progress

- Refocused Buer on everyday assistance, natural conversation, and future gameplay and lore research rather than coding specialization.
- Replaced Granite 3.2 8B with Qwen3.5 9B, running locally through LM Studio and Vulkan on the RX 6650 XT, with reasoning disabled.
- Separated conversation context, LLM communication, and background conversation workers from `main.py` into dedicated modules.
- Disabled automatic experience suggestions to reduce unnecessary background LLM requests while retaining manual memory operations.
- Improved Obsidian retrieval, including follow-up questions, full-document overviews, relevance ranking, and duplicate filtering.
- Removed the dedicated hardware-answer shortcut in favor of a general memory retrieval path.
- Consolidated duplicate hardware records and updated outdated information in the vault.
- Fixed explicit memory commands addressed directly to Buer, preserving approval before writing or editing personal memories.
- Preserved Buer's personality and existing optimized voice system.

## Testing
